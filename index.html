Who I Am

I am an undergraduate Civil Engineering student at the University of Asia Pacific (UAP), based in Bogura. I have a strong foundation in structural mechanics, AutoCAD, Revit, and advanced land surveying (RTK GPS).

My goal is to become a versatile engineer who can design robust structures and develop reliable digital solutions for the real estate and construction industries.

What I Do

Beyond traditional engineering, I actively engage in real estate marketing and land development (e.g., Paikar City, Brigadier Housing Complex). I also build professional web portals using WordPress and utilize Python for engineering analysis.

What I Enjoy

I enjoy learning new technologies, operating drones for aerial site mapping (DJI), exploring AI for development, and blending my technical engineering knowledge with modern web and software development tools.
